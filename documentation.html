⚙️ How to Edit

  • HTML: Open index.html to edit text, sections, and structure.
  • CSS: Open css/style.css to change colors, fonts, spacing, and layout.
  • JavaScript: Open js/script.js to adjust interactivity (e.g., FAQ accordion, smooth scroll).
  • Images: Replace files inside images/ with your own assets.

🎨 Customization

  • Change the primary color by editing the --primary variable in style.css.
  • Replace the logo text in the footer with your brand name.
  • Update social links in the footer with your own URLs.
  • Swap testimonial avatars with your own images.

📱 Responsiveness

The template is fully responsive. It adapts to mobile, tablet, and desktop screens automatically. You can adjust breakpoints in style.css under the @media queries.
